💒 Fintech & e-commerce 28 verified entries

Payment gateways, SDKs and commerce integrations for Bangladesh's financial system — bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, PortWallet and friends. Official vendor SDKs and community wrappers are separated in the table so you know whose code you are trusting, and licence gaps are flagged with ⚠️ because a payment integration with unclear licensing is a compliance problem, not just a technical one. Several popular gateway wrappers have gone quiet; those moved to legacy.md so what remains here is genuinely buildable today. The commercial services themselves (bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz portals) live in the services section.

✨ Editor’s picks

sslcommerz (official)

The official integration collection spanning Laravel, PHP, Flutter, WooCommerce and ASP.NET — when money is involved, vendor-maintained code with a real org behind it beats a community wrapper of unknown provenance. The licence gap is flagged honestly; weigh it before shipping.

The bKash community SDK family

laravel-bkash-tokenize (Laravel), bkashgo (Go), bkash-rs (Rust), bkash-js (web) and flutter-bkash (Flutter) together cover the bKash Tokenized Checkout across the whole modern stack — community-maintained implementations that document exactly how the grant and payment flows work in practice.
