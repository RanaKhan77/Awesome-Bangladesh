🎓 Education & learning 15 verified entries

Learning resources in Bangla and for Bangladeshi learners: system-design courses, interview archives, algorithm visualizers, competitive-programming references and student resource platforms. This is the section where community effort dwarfs institutional output — most of these are maintained by individuals giving back, often alumni writing the guide they wish had existed when they started. Everything is free to access; course-locking and paywalls would disqualify an entry here.

All entries

  • system-design-bangla System design concepts explained in Bangla — scaling, caching, load balancing, the full interview canon, written for readers who think in Bangla. The reference point for serious Bangla-language technical writing. MIT BD-based
  • bangla-programming-resources The long-running collection of Bangla programming tutorials, curated across two decades of Bangla-language tech blogging — a historical archive as much as a learning path. MIT BD-based
  • interview-questions-bangladesh Interview experiences and questions from Bangladeshi tech companies, organised company by company. Useful because it is specific — real rounds from real Dhaka employers, not generic LeetCode wisdom. GPL-3.0 Diaspora
  • AlgorithmVisualizer Interactive algorithm visualizer — watch sorting, searching, graph and dynamic-programming algorithms execute step by step, a teaching tool that makes complexity tangible. MIT Diaspora
  • Competitive-Programming-Reference-Bangla Competitive programming reference written in Bangla — the standard topics of contest programming, from prefix sums to segment trees, in the language many BD contestants actually think in. GPL-3.0 BD-based
  • DL-Sneak-Peek Deep learning resources curated in Bangla by a SUST researcher — notebooks, papers and tutorials mapped for learners entering the field without an English-medium background. Apache-2.0 BD-based
  • montu-mia সিস্টেম ডিজাইন educational toolkit in Bangla — a character-driven, localised take on teaching system design that refuses to be a translated PowerPoint. Custom licence BD-based
  • CSE-Passport Resources for every CSE student in Bangladesh — semester-by-semester guides, books and tool lists shaped around the national curriculum rather than a foreign one. MIT ee list
  • wordpress-developer-guideline-bangla WordPress development guidelines in Bangla — practical, commerce-oriented training material for the platform that powers much of the Bangladeshi web. GPL-3.0 ee list
  • nsu-study-stash Course archive for North South University students — notes, materials and exam preparation organized per course, the kind of archive seniors always keep privately and rarely publish. Custom licence ee list
  • RUET-OJ Online judge used at RUET — a self-hostable contest and practice platform, so any university can run its own judge instead of depending on external sites. MIT Diaspora
  • KonSchool Fuzzy-AHP school recommender for Bangladesh — a research-grounded tool that ranks school choices from multiple criteria instead of reputation alone. AGPL-3.0 Diaspora
  • HSC Stack Curated free resource platform for Bangladesh HSC and SSC students — notes, guides and materials, by the Dhaka-based hscstack org. Open education for the two exams that shape most Bangladeshi students' futures. Apache-2.0 BD-based
  • F1.VISA.USA Practical F-1 visa guide by a Bangladeshi engineer — the process documented by someone who actually went through it, for the thousands who follow the same path each year. MIT BD-based
  • BD-Univeristy-Index Nightly-synced index of Bangladeshi universities with UGC data, developer API and domain verification ⚠ Apache-2.0 declared in README, LICENSE file missing BD-based

✨ Editor’s picks

system-design-bangla

System design explained in Bangla at a depth that matches the English classics — the book-length proof that serious engineering writing does not require English, and the natural first stop for any Bangla-speaking engineer preparing for senior interviews.

interview-questions-bangladesh

Real interview experiences from Bangladeshi tech companies, kept honest by being company-specific rather than generic. It maps the actual landscape of what Dhaka and Chattogram employers ask, which no international interview prep resource can.

How this section is verified

  • Every link is fetched live at verification time — dead links are removed, not kept for history.
  • Bangladesh connections are evidenced (profile location, org page, or the product's own docs) — see the scope rules.
  • Dormant items (3+ years without activity) move to the legacy file with their last-activity date.
  • Licence information is quoted as the publisher states it; gaps are flagged with ⚠️ rather than guessed.
