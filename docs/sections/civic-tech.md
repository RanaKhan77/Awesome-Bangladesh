# 🏢 Civic tech & public services

<img src="../../assets/sections/banner-civic-tech.svg" width="896" alt="🏢 Civic tech & public services banner">

> Part of **[Awesome Bangladesh](../../README.md)** · All section guides: **[index](index.md)**

Technology aimed at public life: government portals, legal information, utility access and citizen services. The centrepiece is Bangla.gov.bd — the state's own Bangla-computing portal with AI assistants, OCR, corpus and fonts, and downloadable datasets and models on its Hugging Face organisation. Everything here is free to use; where a service is run by a government body we say so explicitly, and where a community project fills a gap the state has not, we say that too.

## Editor's picks

### [Bangla.gov.bd](https://bangla.gov.bd/bn)

Kagaj AI writing assistant, Jijnsha AI government-service assistant, Borno OCR, the National Corpus and free fonts — the ICT Division's full-stack bet on Bangla computing, and the first government portal anywhere to treat a language's whole toolchain (models, corpora, OCR, fonts) as public infrastructure. Its Hugging Face org makes the models downloadable and inspectable.

### [desco-prepaid-api](https://github.com/KhBayazidAhmed/desco-prepaid-api)

The purest civic-tech artifact on the list: documentation that exists only because a utility never built it. Reverse-engineering a prepaid meter API and publishing the findings is classic bottom-up civic tech — small, unsanctioned, genuinely useful.
