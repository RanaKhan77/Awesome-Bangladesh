# 🗃️ Datasets & open data

> Part of **[Awesome Bangladesh](../../README.md)** · All section guides: **[index](index.md)**

The data layer of the Bangladeshi open ecosystem: government portals, research corpora on Hugging Face and Kaggle, and international hosts carrying Bangladesh data. Each dataset states its licence where the publisher declares one, and licence-free items are flagged rather than silently listed. Frozen research corpora stay listed because a completed dataset is a finished product, not an abandoned one. When a dataset claims a government source, we check that the source is named and linkable.

## Editor's picks

### [titulm-bangla-corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hishab/titulm-bangla-corpus)

24.3 million documents, 147 GB, CC-BY-4.0 — the largest cleanly licensed Bangla text corpus in existence, published by the Dhaka AI company that trained its own LLM family on it. When the corpus behind a model is this open, everyone's Bangla NLP work gets better.

### [Common Voice Scripted Speech 27.0 (Bengali)](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmu5kut0d00pomh07yay2f34r)

Mozilla's current official release: 1,277 hours of read Bengali speech, CC0, downloadable in one archive. The largest cleanly-licensed Bangla speech corpus available.

## How this section is verified

- Every link is fetched live at verification time — dead links are removed, not kept for history.
- Bangladesh connections are evidenced (profile location, org page, or the product's own docs) — see the [scope rules](../scope-and-eligibility.md).
- Dormant items (3+ years without activity) move to the [legacy file](../../legacy.md) with their last-activity date.
- Licence information is quoted as the publisher states it; gaps are flagged with ⚠️ rather than guessed.
