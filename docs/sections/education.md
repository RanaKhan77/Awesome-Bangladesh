# 🎓 Education & learning

<img src="../../assets/sections/banner-education.svg" width="896" alt="🎓 Education & learning banner">

Learning resources in Bangla and for Bangladeshi learners: system-design courses, interview archives, algorithm visualizers, competitive-programming references and student resource platforms. This is the section where community effort dwarfs institutional output — most of these are maintained by individuals giving back, often alumni writing the guide they wish had existed when they started. Everything is free to access; course-locking and paywalls would disqualify an entry here.

## Editor's picks

### [system-design-bangla](https://github.com/lahin31/system-design-bangla)

System design explained in Bangla at a depth that matches the English classics — the book-length proof that serious engineering writing does not require English, and the natural first stop for any Bangla-speaking engineer preparing for senior interviews.

### [interview-questions-bangladesh](https://github.com/TamimEhsan/interview-questions-bangladesh)

Real interview experiences from Bangladeshi tech companies, kept honest by being company-specific rather than generic. It maps the actual landscape of what Dhaka and Chattogram employers ask, which no international interview prep resource can.

## How this section is verified

- Every link is fetched live at verification time — dead links are removed, not kept for history.
- Bangladesh connections are evidenced (profile location, org page, or the product's own docs) — see the [scope rules](../scope-and-eligibility.md).
- Dormant items (3+ years without activity) move to the [legacy file](../../legacy.md) with their last-activity date.
- Licence information is quoted as the publisher states it; gaps are flagged with ⚠️ rather than guessed.
