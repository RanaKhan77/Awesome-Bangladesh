# 🇧🇩 Bangla language & NLP

> Part of **[Awesome Bangladesh](../../README.md)** · All section guides: **[index](index.md)**

Everything that makes Bangla work on computers: keyboards and input methods, Unicode-Bijoy conversion, normalizers, tokenizers, spell checkers and full NLP stacks. This is the deepest section of Bangladeshi open source — the Avro phonetic lineage alone spans two decades of maintained forks. Licences matter here: several BUET models carry non-commercial terms, and those are flagged with ⚠️ in both the list and the tables below.

## Editor's picks

### **OpenBangla Keyboard**

The flagship consumer Bangla input method — cross-platform, actively maintained, and the direct descendant of the original Avro that defined phonetic typing in Bangladesh.

### **bnnormalizer / normalizer (csebuetnlp)**

Text normalization looks trivial until you handle Bengali numeral forms, vowel signs and punctuation mixing. These utilities are the quiet foundation under most Bangla NLP pipelines.
